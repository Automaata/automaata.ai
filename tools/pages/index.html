Automation

Automate everything. Decide the important things yourself.

Every action Automaata takes carries one of four levels. The levels are fixed by what the action touches, not by how busy you are.

Level 0

Drafts and files

Work that produces a document for a person to use.

  • Report drafts and summaries
  • Daily activity reports
  • Reminders and notices

Level 1

Executes and logs

Routine actions that are reversible and fully recorded.

  • Publishing an unedited tour schedule
  • Sending routine client reports
  • Filing a checkpoint exception

Level 2

Proposes, you approve

Anything that changes who works, how much they earn or what a client pays.

  • Shift assignments and overtime
  • Pay-run approval
  • Invoice release

Level 3

Never automated

Decisions that belong to a manager and a conversation.

  • Discipline
  • Termination
  • Licensing determinations

Automated scheduling and pay decisions are regulated. Automaata is designed around notice before use, a human review path and a record of every decision, so your operation can meet California's automated decision-making rules and similar frameworks as they come into force.
