Company overview

Built for the people who protect what matters most.

Automaata is the platform behind the officers on post and the people running the operation behind them. It was started by someone who has done that job, at venues where the roster changes every day and the client wants proof by morning.

Who we are

Operators first. Software second.

Automaata was founded in San Francisco in 2026 out of years of running event and contract security at the Bay Area's largest venues: daily rosters of hundreds, badge checks at the door, timesheets reconciled against a client's settlement report before an invoice could go out. The platform exists because that work should not depend on a clipboard and a spreadsheet at 6 a.m.

Protect what matters

Visibility and control that keep people and places safe, built from what officers actually do on a shift.

Respect the people

Tools for the officer on post and the supervisor in the office that take work off their hands instead of adding a form.

Everywhere it counts

The same standard in a convention hall, a hospital corridor or a parking garage with no signal.

What we believe

The values behind the platform

Safety is the mission

Our customers' work is serious, and the software should carry that weight: a report may become evidence, and a schedule decides someone's pay.

Automate the work, not the judgment

Four automation levels say exactly what the platform does on its own and what waits for a person. The line is fixed by consequences, not convenience.

Trust through proof

AI-written text is labeled. Every automated action has an audit record. Client data never trains a model. We claim certifications only after they are audited.
