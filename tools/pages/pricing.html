Questions about Automaata pricing

How do I get Automaata pricing?

Use the request form above or email hello@automaata.ai with your roster size, number of sites and the modules you want. A specialist replies within one business day.

What information is needed to prepare a quote?

Roughly how many officers you schedule, how many sites or posts, which state you operate in, and which modules you want to start with. If you have a current tool, tell us which; it shapes migration.

Can I start with a few modules and add more later?

Yes. Many operations start with checkpoint tours and incident reporting, then add scheduling and billing. The modules share one record, so nothing is re-entered when you add one.

How long does implementation take?

Sites and posts first, then officers and checkpoints, then scheduling and billing once field data is flowing. We set the timeline with you during the demo; implementation and officer onboarding are included.

Is there a minimum, or a long-term contract?

We quote per officer per month and do not require a long-term commitment to start. Annual terms are available if you prefer a fixed price.

Does Automaata integrate with payroll, accounting and security systems?

Payroll-ready exports to ADP, QuickBooks and Paychex; an API with per-entity schemas; and a dispatch queue that video analytics and alarm systems can publish into.

What security certifications does Automaata hold?

SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 are on the roadmap and will be claimed only once audited. The commitments in force today, including encryption, per-customer isolation, no model training on client data and audit records for every automated action, are on the Trust page.

Can I see Automaata before I buy?

Yes. The request form books a 30-minute walkthrough on your own sites. Bring a schedule, a post order and a month of invoices.
