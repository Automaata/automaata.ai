Automation levels

How Automaata decides what to do on its own

Every action in the platform carries a level, set by what the action touches rather than by how busy the office is.

Level 0

Drafts and files

Reports, DARs, summaries, reminders.

Level 1

Executes and logs

Reversible routine actions: an unedited tour schedule, a routine client report, a checkpoint exception.

Level 2

Proposes, you approve

Shift assignments, overtime, pay runs, invoice release.

Level 3

Never automated

Discipline, termination, licensing determinations.

Your questions, answered

What is Automaata?

Security workforce management software with the paperwork built in. An officer app, an operations console and a client portal share one record, AI agents draft and execute routine work, and an approval inbox holds the decisions that touch pay or people for a person to sign off.

Who is it designed for?

Contract guard companies (event security, standing posts, mobile patrol) and in-house corporate security departments. The data model was designed around event work with daily rosters of hundreds, so it handles multi-day shows as comfortably as a single lobby post.

Does it work without a phone signal?

Yes. The Officer App stores scans, reports, photos and clock punches on the device with a timestamp and sequence number, then syncs when a signal returns. Nothing is lost and nothing is double-counted.

What does the AI actually do, and what does it not do?

It drafts reports, DARs and summaries (Level 0), executes reversible routine actions (Level 1) and proposes schedules, overtime, pay runs and invoices for a person to approve (Level 2). It never makes discipline, termination or licensing decisions (Level 3). AI-written text is labeled, the original note is kept, and every action has an audit record.

How long does implementation take?

Sites and posts first, then officers and checkpoints, then scheduling and billing once field data is flowing. Officer onboarding is designed to take under ten minutes. We scope the timeline with you during the demo and implementation is included in the price.

How much does it cost?

Per officer per month, for the modules you run, with implementation included. Request pricing and a specialist replies within one business day with a quote and a 30-minute walkthrough.

Does it integrate with payroll and accounting?

Payroll-ready exports to ADP, QuickBooks and Paychex, plus an API with per-entity schemas for everything else. Video analytics and alarm systems can publish events into the dispatch queue.

Can we see it before buying?

Yes. Book a demo and bring a schedule, a post order and a month of invoices. You will see what Automaata drafts, what it executes and what it hands back for a decision, using your own sites.
