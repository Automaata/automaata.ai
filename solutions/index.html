By industry

Where Automaata is built to work

The same record, configured for the way each kind of site actually runs.

Event & venue security

Convention centers, arenas, piers

Daily rosters of hundreds, credential checks at the door, multi-day shows and settlement reconciliation with the venue's own time system.

Corporate campuses

Lobbies, badge desks, patrols

One incident taxonomy across locations, visitor logs, and reporting that satisfies legal and compliance.

Hospitality

Hotels and resorts

Overnight coverage, per-incident forms clients require, and guest-facing discretion in every report.

Residential communities

Gates, patrols, post orders

Versioned post orders with read receipts, patrol tours by NFC and GPS, and resident-facing incident summaries.

Logistics & industrial

Yards, warehouses, plants

Perimeter GPS checkpoints, lone-worker safety for night shifts, and gate logs that tie to shipments.

Healthcare

Hospitals and clinics

After-hours coverage, de-escalation incident categories, and records formatted for risk and compliance reviews.

One connected platform behind every solution

One record

From shift to invoice

Field hours flow into payroll and billing without re-keying. A scan in a garage at 2 a.m. is on the client's invoice line in the same record.

At the source

Data captured where it happens

Officers capture it on the phone, with or without signal. There is no end-of-month consolidation because there is nothing to consolidate.

Governed

Automation with a line you can see

Four levels decide what runs itself and what waits for you. Every automated action has an audit record, and AI-written text is labeled.
